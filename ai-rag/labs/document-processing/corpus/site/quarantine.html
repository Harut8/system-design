Why quarantine beats a dead letter queue

A dead letter queue accumulates documents nobody looks at, because nothing in the queue says what to do about them.

Attaching the gate verdict to each quarantined document converts the pile into a work list sorted by which parser change unblocks the most documents.

The metric that matters is not quarantine depth but quarantine age, because a shallow queue nobody drains is the same failure as a deep one.
