The delete you skipped

Upsert works and deletes are extra code, so deletes are the step that gets skipped, and nothing visibly breaks on the day you skip them.

Content removed from a document but left in the index is retrieved and cited as current, with a source link that no longer contains it.

That is worse than missing data, because it is confidently wrong data carrying a citation that survives casual checking.
